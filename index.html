kaitlin hsu 徐欣 is a queer taiwanese writer and editor from the bay area. she’s the author of the chapbook paper dolls (sibling rivalry press, 2026). she studied english (poetry) and computer science (biocomputation) at stanford and is currently a poetry mfa candidate at the michener center.
